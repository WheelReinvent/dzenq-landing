How it works inside

DzenQ is built on the open KERI protocol: everyone has their own self-certifying identifier and key log, with no blockchain and no central registry. Each thank-you is a signed ACDC container. Doors check the proof themselves, even offline, with a one-time code. Websites add "Sign in with DzenQ" as plain OpenID Connect.
